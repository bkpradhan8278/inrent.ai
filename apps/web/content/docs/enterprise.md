## Available today

- Organizations with roles (Owner, Admin, Developer, Billing, Viewer) and invitations.
- Projects with monthly budgets and model allowlists.
- Organization spending caps and per-key limits.
- Audit logs and data exports.
- Prompt and response logging controls, configurable retention and zero-retention mode.
- Custom routing policies and provider pinning.
- Enterprise provider agreements, served only to the organizations they cover.

## Roadmap

- SAML/OIDC SSO and SCIM provisioning.
- Dedicated gateway capacity and self-hosted model endpoints.
- Data residency and private networking.
- Invoicing and committed-spend contracts.

INRENT does not currently hold third-party certifications such as SOC 2 or ISO 27001.
